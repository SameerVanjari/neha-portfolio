# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js 16, React 19, Tailwind CSS v4, Framer Motion, Barba-style page masks, GSAP (optional)

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, and design directors evaluating Neha for 2026 STEM OPT roles and collaborations.

## Product Purpose

Neha Mayacharya's portfolio — an interactive journey through four equal perceptions (XR, UX, AI, Product). The site makes it fast to see what she has shipped, for whom, and whether to engage.

## Positioning

A calm, authoritative portfolio that lets **real work** lead. Perception switching is the distinctive mechanic; it never replaces named clients, outcomes, or case depth.

## Operating Context

Desktop-first, 390px–1920px+. Light studio default with four tonal washes. Visitor scans identity → work → process → proof → contact.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Content lives in `src/data/portfolio.json`
- Four perceptions remain equal in the island nav; project counts per lens may be uneven
- Case studies are moderate: challenge / approach / result + Behance link — not long essays
- Stills, video, Figma, and 3D assets will replace Unsplash placeholders later
- Code/developer skills stay in the toolkit, not the headline
- Preserve perception island, clay icons, and color-wave transitions

## Brand Commitments

- Name: NEHA
- Tagline: Perception, powered by design + AI
- Dimensions: PRODUCT (cyan), UX (volt/gold), XR (neon/pink), AI (sigil/purple)
- Tone: Confident, precise, calm — never salesy

## Evidence on Hand

- `src/data/portfolio.json` — profile, real projects, clients, recognition, experience
- `src/app/` — `/`, `/projects`, `/projects/[id]`, `/about`
- `public/profile/` — headshot, logo
- `public/clay/` — island mascots (placeholders until final 3D)

## Product Principles

1. The work leads — every pixel serves the content
2. Calm authority — controlled, not frantic
3. Recruiter scan in under 10 seconds: name, availability, clients, work
4. Craft over spectacle — perception effects serve orientation
5. Honest proof — no invented testimonials or fictional case studies
